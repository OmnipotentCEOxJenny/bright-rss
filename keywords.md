The same 40 keywords are stored in `.cursor-plugin/plugin.json` (`keywords`). They follow Rob's keyword rules (/workspace/keyword-rules.md, Oct 22, 2026): single words lowercase, joined words PascalCase, no hyphens, spaces or commas, specific to Bright RSS; the only shared ones are BrightRss, JennyEcho and OmnipotentCeo. Joined words use PascalCase (rule 7); single words stay lowercase.
